Imputation accuracy across the allele-frequency spectrum. Rows are datasets, columns are the fraction of genotypes masked. Each line is the median over the 22 autosomes of the phased four-class R2 pooled within a chromosome, averaged over three masking seeds, and the band around it spans the full range over those chromosomes. The two BiU-Net series differ only in whether the genome-wide model was fine-tuned on one chromosome for 30 epochs, so the vertical distance between them is the effect of that fine-tuning.

*Source: benchmark cells in analysis/. Code: scripts/plot_accuracy_by_frequency.py*

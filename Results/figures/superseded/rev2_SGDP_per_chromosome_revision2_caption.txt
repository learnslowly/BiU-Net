Per-chromosome imputation accuracy on SGDP. Panels (a), (b) and (c) show 5%, 15%, 25% of genotypes masked. Each point is the phased four-class $R^2$ pooled over all positions of one chromosome, averaged over masking seeds [0, 42, 1024]. Lines join chromosomes for readability and carry no interpolation.

*Source: benchmark cells in analysis/ for dataset SGDP. Code: scripts/build_rev2_figures.py*
